Goods and Services Tax (GST) is India's unified indirect tax system. This guide covers everything you need to know about calculating GST correctly.

## GST Rate Slabs

India has four primary GST rates: 5%, 12%, 18%, and 28%. Most goods and services fall under the 18% standard rate.

## GST Exclusive Calculation

When the price doesn't include GST:

- GST Amount = Base Price × Rate / 100
- Total Price = Base Price + GST Amount

**Example:** ₹10,000 + 18% GST = ₹10,000 + ₹1,800 = ₹11,800

## GST Inclusive Calculation

When the price already includes GST:

- Base Price = Inclusive Price / (1 + Rate/100)
- GST Amount = Inclusive Price - Base Price

**Example:** ₹11,800 inclusive at 18% = ₹11,800 / 1.18 = ₹10,000 base + ₹1,800 GST

## CGST and SGST

For intra-state sales, GST splits equally: 9% CGST + 9% SGST = 18% total. For inter-state sales, 18% IGST applies.
