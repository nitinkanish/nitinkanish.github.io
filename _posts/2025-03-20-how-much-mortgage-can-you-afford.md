Buying a home is exciting, but affordability should drive your budget. A bigger sanctioned loan is not always the right loan for your lifestyle and future goals.

## Rule of Thumb for Affordability

Many advisors suggest keeping total housing costs within 28-35% of monthly net income. This includes:

- EMI
- Property tax
- Insurance
- Maintenance charges

## Factors That Affect Mortgage Eligibility

- Monthly income and job stability
- Existing EMIs or debt obligations
- Credit score and repayment history
- Down payment amount
- Loan tenure and interest rate

## Why Tenure Matters

Longer tenure reduces monthly EMI but increases lifetime interest outgo. A shorter tenure can save significant interest if your cash flow allows it.

## Plan for Hidden Costs

Beyond EMI, include:

- Registration and stamp duty
- Furnishing and moving costs
- Emergency fund after purchase

These costs can strain finances if ignored.

## Safe Borrowing Strategy

- Keep a buffer for unexpected expenses
- Avoid maxing out lender-approved eligibility
- Recalculate affordability before finalizing the property
