Body Mass Index (BMI) is the most widely used screening tool for weight categories. Here's everything you need to know about how it's calculated and what it means.

## The BMI Formula

```
BMI = weight (kg) / height (m)²
```

For imperial units: BMI = (weight in pounds × 703) / (height in inches)²

## BMI Categories

| Category | BMI Range |
|----------|-----------|
| Underweight | Below 18.5 |
| Normal | 18.5 – 24.9 |
| Overweight | 25 – 29.9 |
| Obese | 30 and above |

## Example Calculation

A person weighing 70 kg with height 170 cm (1.70 m):
BMI = 70 / (1.70 × 1.70) = 70 / 2.89 = **24.2** (Normal weight)

## Limitations

BMI doesn't measure body fat directly. Athletes with high muscle mass may be classified as overweight despite being healthy. Always consult healthcare professionals for comprehensive health assessment.
