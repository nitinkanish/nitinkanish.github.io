A Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) is one of the most powerful wealth-building tools available to Indian investors. This comprehensive guide explains everything you need to know about SIP investing.

## What is a SIP?

A SIP is a method of investing a fixed amount at regular intervals (usually monthly) in a mutual fund. Instead of investing ₹1,20,000 as a lump sum, you might invest ₹10,000 every month for 12 months.

## How SIP Returns Are Calculated

Each monthly investment compounds independently. The formula for SIP future value is:

```
FV = P × ((1+r)^n - 1) / r × (1+r)
```

Where P is monthly investment, r is monthly return rate, and n is number of months.

## Rupee Cost Averaging

When markets fall, your fixed SIP amount buys more mutual fund units. When markets rise, you buy fewer units. Over time, this averages your purchase cost and reduces the impact of market volatility.

## Step-Up SIP Strategy

Increasing your SIP by 10% annually can dramatically accelerate wealth creation. A ₹5,000 SIP stepped up by 10% yearly for 20 years at 12% returns can build a corpus exceeding ₹1 crore.

## Common SIP Mistakes to Avoid

- Stopping SIP during market downturns (this defeats rupee cost averaging)
- Choosing funds based only on recent performance
- Not aligning SIP with specific financial goals
- Ignoring expense ratios and exit loads
