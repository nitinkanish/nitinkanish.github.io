Percentages are used in shopping, academics, finance, and business. Once you know a few core formulas, most percentage questions become easy.

## Core Percentage Formulas

- Percentage = `(Part / Whole) x 100`
- Percentage increase = `((New - Old) / Old) x 100`
- Percentage decrease = `((Old - New) / Old) x 100`

## Discount Example

If a shirt costs Rs. 2,000 and has a 25% discount:

- Discount = 2,000 x 25 / 100 = Rs. 500
- Final price = Rs. 2,000 - Rs. 500 = Rs. 1,500

## Markup Example

If cost price is Rs. 800 and markup is 30%:

- Markup = 800 x 30 / 100 = Rs. 240
- Selling price = Rs. 1,040

## Exam Percentage Example

If you score 420 out of 500:

- Percentage = (420 / 500) x 100 = 84%

## Common Mistake to Avoid

Do not add or subtract percentage points incorrectly. A 20% increase followed by 20% decrease does not bring you back to the original value.
